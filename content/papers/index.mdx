Every lesson and glossary entry in this course eventually points back to a
paper — the actual publication where an idea was first shown to work. This
section is short explainers for the ones cited most often: what problem the
authors were solving, the key idea in plain language, and why it ended up
mattering enough to reshape the field. Not a summary of the abstract — this
site already has [lessons](/docs) and a [glossary](/reference) for that;
these pages are about the paper itself as a historical and technical event.

Each page links out to the paper on arXiv for anyone who wants to read the
real thing. Grouped roughly by topic in the sidebar. This is a seed list, not
a complete literature review — more will be added as the course grows.
