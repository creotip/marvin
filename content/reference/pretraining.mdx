---
title: Pretraining
description: Pretraining is self-supervised training of a base LLM on massive amounts of text to predict the next token, the primary source of its knowledge and language ability.
---

**Pretraining** is self-supervised training (no human-labeled data
needed) of an [LLM](/reference/llm) on a massive text corpus: predict the
next token at every position, using the actual next word in real text as
the label. This is the primary source of a model's knowledge, grammar,
and reasoning patterns. The result is a **base model** — fluent, but not
naturally instruction-following — which is why
[fine-tuning](/reference/fine-tuning) and [RLHF](/reference/rlhf) follow.
