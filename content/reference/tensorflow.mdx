**TensorFlow** (Google) was the dominant [deep learning](/reference/deep-learning)
framework in the mid-2010s. It's still widely used in existing production
systems and in mobile/edge deployment (via TensorFlow Lite), but has lost
most of the research and new-project share to [PyTorch](/reference/pytorch)
over the last several years. **Keras** is TensorFlow's high-level model
API.
