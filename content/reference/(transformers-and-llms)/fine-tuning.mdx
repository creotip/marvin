---
title: Fine-tuning
description: Fine-tuning continues training a pretrained model on a smaller, curated dataset to teach it a specific behavior, such as following instructions.
---

**Fine-tuning** takes a [pretrained](/reference/pretraining) base model
and continues training it on a smaller, curated dataset shaped like the
target behavior — e.g. `(instruction, ideal response)` pairs for
**supervised fine-tuning (SFT)**. It's far cheaper than pretraining since
the model already has language and knowledge; fine-tuning mostly teaches
*behavior*, not new facts. **PEFT** methods like **LoRA** fine-tune by
training a small number of additional parameters instead of the whole
model, making fine-tuning practical even for very large models.
