A **large language model (LLM)** is a large [transformer](/reference/transformer)
trained in a multi-stage recipe: [pretraining](/reference/pretraining) on
huge amounts of text to predict the next token, then
[fine-tuning](/reference/fine-tuning) (often with [RLHF](/reference/rlhf))
to follow instructions and match human preferences. Examples include GPT,
Claude, Gemini, and Llama. Unlike earlier AI systems built one-per-task,
a single LLM can be prompted into translation, coding, summarization, and
many other tasks without task-specific training.
