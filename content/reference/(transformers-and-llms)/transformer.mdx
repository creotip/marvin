The **transformer** is the neural network architecture introduced in the
2017 paper "Attention Is All You Need," built entirely around
**self-attention** — letting every position in a sequence directly weigh
every other position, regardless of distance. It replaced older sequence
models (RNNs) because it's parallelizable to train, handles long-range
dependencies directly, and scales predictably with size and data. It
underlies essentially every modern [LLM](/reference/llm), and
increasingly vision and audio models too.
