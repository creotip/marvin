**RoPE (Rotary Positional Embedding)** is a
[positional encoding](/reference/positional-encoding) scheme used in most
current [LLMs](/reference/llm). Instead of adding a positional vector to
each token's embedding, it encodes position by *rotating* the Query and
Key vectors used in [attention](/reference/attention) by an amount
depending on their position in the sequence. It tends to generalize
better to sequence lengths longer than what the model was trained on,
which matters for long-context models.
