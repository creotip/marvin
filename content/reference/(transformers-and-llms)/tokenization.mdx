title: Tokenization

**Tokenization** converts raw text into a sequence of integer **tokens**
a [neural network](/reference/neural-network) can process. Modern
[LLMs](/reference/llm) use **subword tokenization** (commonly
**byte-pair encoding**, BPE): common words get a single token, rarer
words split into smaller frequent pieces — keeping sequences short while
still representing any string, including unseen words. Each token is
then mapped to a learned [embedding](/reference/embedding) vector before
entering the model.
