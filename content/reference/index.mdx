Short, linkable definitions for terms used throughout [the course](/docs).
Each entry links to the lesson that covers it in depth, related terms, and
an external canonical source. Use this when you hit an unfamiliar term and
want a quick answer instead of a full lesson.

Browse alphabetically in the sidebar, or jump to a term directly at
`/reference/<term>` — e.g. [`/reference/transformer`](/reference/transformer).
