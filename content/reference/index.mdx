Short, linkable definitions for terms used throughout [the course](/docs).
Each entry links to the lesson that covers it in depth, related terms, and
an external canonical source. Use this when you hit an unfamiliar term and
want a quick answer instead of a full lesson.

Browse the [full alphabetical index](/reference/a-z), browse by topic in
the sidebar, or jump to a term directly at `/reference/<term>` — e.g.
[`/reference/transformer`](/reference/transformer).

Have a broader question that doesn't map to one term or lesson — like
whether RAG is the same as fine-tuning? Check the [FAQ](/faq).
