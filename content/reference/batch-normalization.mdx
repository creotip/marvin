---
title: Batch/Layer Normalization
description: Batch and layer normalization rescale a layer's outputs during training to keep values well-behaved as they pass through many stacked layers.
---

**Batch normalization** and **layer normalization** rescale a layer's
outputs (to roughly mean 0, variance 1) during training, preventing
values from drifting too large or small as they pass through many
stacked layers — a direct countermeasure to
[vanishing/exploding gradients](/reference/vanishing-gradients). Batch
normalization normalizes across a batch of examples; **layer
normalization** normalizes across a single example's features instead,
and is the variant used throughout [transformers](/reference/transformer).
