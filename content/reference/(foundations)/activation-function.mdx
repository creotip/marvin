An **activation function** is the nonlinear function applied after a
[neural network](/reference/neural-network) neuron's weighted sum —
without it, stacking layers would collapse into one large linear function,
no more expressive than a single layer. **ReLU** (`max(0, z)`) is the
standard modern default: cheap, and its derivative doesn't shrink for
positive inputs, avoiding much of the [vanishing gradient](/reference/vanishing-gradients)
problem that plagued older choices like **sigmoid** and **tanh**. **GELU**,
a smoother ReLU variant, is used in most modern transformers.
