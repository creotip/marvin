**Artificial intelligence (AI)** is the broad field concerned with
building systems that perform tasks normally requiring human intelligence:
reasoning, perception, language understanding, and decision-making. It's
an umbrella term — [machine learning](/reference/machine-learning),
[deep learning](/reference/deep-learning), and today's
[LLMs](/reference/llm) are all specific approaches *within* AI, not
synonyms for it. Symbolic, rule-based systems (expert systems) are also
AI, even though they contain no learning at all.
