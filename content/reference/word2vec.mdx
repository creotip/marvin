**word2vec** (2013) was a technique for learning dense vector
representations of words directly from raw text — words used in similar
contexts end up with similar vectors, learned without manual labeling.
It was a major step in bringing [deep learning](/reference/deep-learning)
into language processing, and a direct precursor to the token
[embeddings](/reference/embedding) used throughout modern
[LLMs](/reference/llm).
