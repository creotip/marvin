A **GPU (Graphics Processing Unit)**, originally built for rendering
graphics, turned out to be extremely well-suited to the large parallel
matrix multiplications [deep learning](/reference/deep-learning) needs —
one of the three factors (alongside data and algorithms) that made deep
learning practical starting around 2012. NVIDIA GPUs paired with
[CUDA](/reference/cuda) are the dominant combination for both training
and [inference](/reference/inference); **TPUs** (Google) and AMD's ROCm
are the main alternatives.
