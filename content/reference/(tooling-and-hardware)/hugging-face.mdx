title: Hugging Face
description: Hugging Face is an ecosystem — transformers, datasets, and the Hub — providing pretrained models and standardized tooling on top of frameworks like PyTorch.
---

**Hugging Face** is the ecosystem layer most practitioners build on top
of a framework like [PyTorch](/reference/pytorch): the `transformers`
library provides thousands of pretrained model architectures with a
consistent load/fine-tune/run API; the `datasets` library and the
**Hugging Face Hub** give standardized access to public datasets and
pretrained weights. `accelerate` and `PEFT` (including **LoRA**) handle
multi-GPU distribution and efficient [fine-tuning](/reference/fine-tuning)
respectively.
