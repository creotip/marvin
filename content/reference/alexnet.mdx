**AlexNet** is the deep [CNN](/reference/cnn) — built by Alex Krizhevsky,
Ilya Sutskever, and Geoffrey Hinton — that won the 2012 ImageNet
competition by a wide margin, roughly halving the previous best error
rate. Trained on GPUs with ReLU activations, it's widely credited as the
moment that proved deep learning could dramatically outperform
hand-engineered feature pipelines, kicking off the deep learning boom of
the 2010s.
