**Dario Amodei** led safety-focused research at OpenAI, including early
work on scaling laws — the empirical finding that a model's performance
improves predictably as a smooth function of parameters, data, and
compute, which became a central argument for why scale itself was worth
pursuing aggressively. In 2021, alongside his sister
[Daniela Amodei](/people/daniela-amodei) and several other OpenAI
researchers, he co-founded Anthropic, built around the premise that AI
safety research is most effective when done by a team also building and
shipping frontier models, rather than studied at arm's length from labs
racing ahead on capability alone.

As Anthropic's CEO, Amodei has pushed [RLHF](/reference/rlhf)-style
alignment techniques and interpretability research (trying to understand
what's actually happening inside a trained model, not just how it behaves
at the input-output level) as core research priorities alongside model
capability, developing the Claude family of models. He has written
extensively and publicly about both the transformative potential of
powerful AI and the risks of getting safety wrong at frontier scale,
positioning Anthropic's mission explicitly around that tension rather than
treating it as a side concern.
