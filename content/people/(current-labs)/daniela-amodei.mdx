**Daniela Amodei** co-founded Anthropic in 2021 with her brother
[Dario Amodei](/people/dario-amodei) and a group of researchers who left
OpenAI over disagreements about how safety-focused the path to frontier
AI should be. Before Anthropic, she held policy and operations roles at
OpenAI and Stripe, experience that shaped her role as Anthropic's
president — running the parts of a frontier AI lab that determine whether
its safety mission survives contact with the pressure to ship: trust and
safety policy, operations, and how the company engages with regulators
and the public as AI capabilities scale quickly.

Anthropic's central bet — that a lab can stay commercially competitive at
the frontier while treating safety and interpretability as core research
rather than an afterthought — depends as much on how the organization is
run as on the research itself. Daniela Amodei's role has been building
that organization: scaling Anthropic from a small research team into a
company shipping the Claude model family, while keeping its structure
(including a long-term benefit trust designed to weight the company's
mission over pure commercial return) aligned with the safety-first
premise it was founded on.
