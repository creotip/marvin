Every idea in [the course](/docs) and [the glossary](/reference) has a name
attached to it — someone who proved a theorem, shipped a paper, or bet a
career on an approach everyone else had given up on. This section is short
biographical profiles of those people: what they actually did, why it
mattered, and where it shows up in the curriculum.

Grouped roughly by era in the sidebar — founding theory, the connectionist
revival, the modern deep learning and transformer era, and the labs and
applied work shaping AI today. Names mentioned in lessons and glossary
entries link here automatically, the same way technical terms link to the
glossary.

This site is named for one of them —
[Marvin Minsky](/people/marvin-minsky), a founder of the field who spent
his career arguing about what intelligence actually is.
